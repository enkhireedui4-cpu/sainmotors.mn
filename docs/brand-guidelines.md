# Sain Motors website brand guide

The source company guide specifies UI red **#E20A17**, Zona lettering and black/red, white/red or white logos. The latest user preference keeps the system/Inter typeface for interface text; Zona is retained in the S/name artwork. Supplied logo geometry and its original red remain untouched.

## Palette and roles

| Role | Colour | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Sain red | `#e20a17` | Primary actions, current navigation, selected filters, short section signatures |
| Red hover | `#ba0813` | Hover/pressed primary action |
| White | `#ffffff` | Content canvas, text on black/red |
| Black | `#000000` | Page arrivals, brand presentation, footer |
| Ink | `#101113` | Main text and information cards |
| Neutral surface | `#f6f6f6` | Supporting cards/panels |
| Muted text | `#626262` | Secondary text on white |
| Divider | `#dedede` | Borders and hairlines |

Tokens live in `src/app/brand-tokens.css`. Existing semantic variables alias them. Do not add a new red or blue-gray for a corporate UI component. Real car photographs, brand logos and map imagery keep their own colours.

## Shared treatments

- Primary actions: red background, white text, pill radius, at least 48px height. Secondary actions remain outlined or underlined. Hover and focus remain visible.
- Filters: red/white selected state. On black surfaces, inactive text remains white. Long Mongolian labels wrap rather than clip.
- Page arrivals: black/white, shared title scale and short red signature. Long news headlines retain the narrower left-aligned editorial column.
- Navigation: red current/hover underline; the home header uses white/red artwork over its film, then black/red after scrolling. Mobile active state and phone action use red.
- Main content: shared gutter and 90rem maximum; article columns, maps and cinematic imagery intentionally have their own proportions.
- Cards/images: 12px radius. Circular controls and pill labels keep their functional geometry.
- Footer: black with official white/red logo and thin red top edge.
- Copy: management-supplied facts, bilingual date precision and current curated images remain authoritative. No duplicate About motto/description block after the user's final removal request.

Use generous spacing, complete Mongolian glyph coverage, 15–18px body copy and restrained red accents. Do not paint every section red, tint partner artwork, or introduce automatic decorative motion.
