# Dark Brand Catalogue

## Design

The Brands page opens with its localized title on black, followed directly by the grid. Eleven charcoal panels show a centered white wordmark, a visible name, a bilingual introduction and one destination. Use the supplied name-only crops for BESTUNE, HONGQI and MAXUS and a consistent logo height. Use Sain Motors typography and red accents. Arrange the panels in three, two and one columns as space allows.
