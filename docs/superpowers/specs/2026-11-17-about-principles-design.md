# About principles redesign

The user delegated the design direction and supplied a staggered black/red card reference. Replace the existing four full-bleed purpose panels and their two car photographs/duplicate showroom film. Preserve all bilingual mission, vision, values and development copy. In the follow-up, the user also requested removal of the three motto photographs and integration of the motto into this composition.

## Direction

Use a white canvas, large left-aligned Inter heading and four independently sized cards. Mission, vision and values use black; development uses Sain red. Pale continuous road curves connect the composition visually. Four small line symbols communicate each card's subject. No photographs, new dependencies, automatic motion or click-like affordances on informational cards.

Palette: paper #ffffff, ink #101113, muted ink #c7c8cb, route #e8e9eb, Sain red #c90019. Body 15–18px, card titles 24–32px, section heading 36–72px. Maintain clear contrast, generous text padding and natural card growth. Desktop uses a staggered 12-column composition; tablet uses two columns; mobile uses four cards in reading order.

The integrated introduction pairs the large left-aligned motto with its management-supplied description and two onward links on the right. It shares the same white canvas, road curves, gutters and responsive rules as the cards. Preserve the existing scroll word fill and its reduced-motion/no-JS fallback; remove the three car-photo elements and their positioning CSS.

An equal four-tile grid was considered but would retain the current rigid layout. A photographic composition conflicts with the requested removal of the three visuals. The chosen composition follows the supplied visual direction while using Sain's palette and existing typeface.
